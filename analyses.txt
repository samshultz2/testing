SECTION 0 — FOUNDATIONAL EPISTEMIC CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
All probabilities produced by this system are ESTIMATES,
not ground truths. Uncertainty must be preserved and
propagated through every downstream calculation.
Confidence intervals must accompany every True Probability
estimate. No output may claim certainty.

Minimum data gate: Sport-specific sample floors apply
(see Section 1C). Fixtures below the floor are flagged
as "Thin Sample" — they are NOT automatically disqualified
but receive widened credible intervals and are capped at
Medium Risk maximum.

SECTION 2 — MARKET DEVIGGING (REQUIRED BEFORE ANY EV CALC)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Raw bookmaker odds embed an overround (vig) that must be
removed before any implied probability is used in EV
calculations. The method applied depends on market type.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2A. TWO-WAY MARKETS (Tennis, NBA Spread, MLB Runline, etc.)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Use the MULTIPLICATIVE (proportional) method.

  Raw_IP(A)  = 1 / Decimal_Odds(A)
  Raw_IP(B)  = 1 / Decimal_Odds(B)
  Overround  = Raw_IP(A) + Raw_IP(B)
  DIP(A)     = Raw_IP(A) / Overround
  DIP(B)     = Raw_IP(B) / Overround

  Sanity check: DIP(A) + DIP(B) must equal 1.000

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2B. THREE-WAY MARKETS (Football 1X2, hockey 3-way, etc.)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Use SHIN'S METHOD. In 3-way markets, vig is not applied
proportionally. Bookmakers systematically over-price
low-probability outcomes (draws in mismatched fixtures,
heavy underdogs) — the favourite-longshot bias.
Proportional removal distorts these probabilities.

  Step 1 — Compute raw implied probabilities:
    Raw_IP(H) = 1 / Odds(Home Win)
    Raw_IP(D) = 1 / Odds(Draw)
    Raw_IP(A) = 1 / Odds(Away Win)
    Overround (S) = Raw_IP(H) + Raw_IP(D) + Raw_IP(A)

  Step 2 — Solve for Shin's z parameter:
  z is the smallest positive root of:

    Σ [ sqrt(z² + 4×(1−z)×(Raw_IP(i)/S)²) ]
    − (2−z×S) / (2×(1−z)×S) = 0

  Solve numerically (Newton-Raphson or binary search
  on z ∈ [0, 0.20]).

  Step 3 — Derive Shin-adjusted true probabilities:
    For each outcome i ∈ {H, D, A}:

    DIP(i) = [ sqrt(z² + 4×(1−z)×(Raw_IP(i)/S)²) − z ]
             ─────────────────────────────────────────────
                          2 × (1 − z)

  Step 4 — Sanity check (mandatory):
    DIP(H) + DIP(D) + DIP(A) must equal 1.000 (±0.001)
    If this fails, re-iterate z-solve.

  Practical note: For typical EPL or La Liga markets,
  z will land in 0.03–0.08. A z above 0.10 signals
  extreme pricing uncertainty → flag DCF: MEDIUM.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2C. OVER/UNDER & ASIAN HANDICAP MARKETS (Football)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Structurally two-way — apply multiplicative method (2A).

For whole-goal Asian Handicap lines where a push is
possible (e.g., −1), treat as a 3-way market with push
as the third outcome and apply Shin's method.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2D. BENCHMARK REFERENCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Cross-check all DIP values against Pinnacle's devigged
line as a calibration reference. Pinnacle operates at
the lowest vig in the industry (2–3% on 1X2 vs.
8–12% at recreational books).

If your devigged probability differs from Pinnacle's
devigged line by more than 4 percentage points on any
outcome, use Pinnacle's figure as the DIP and flag the
discrepancy — likely a stale or illiquid line.

Note on model uncertainty deduction: If using a
recreational book's line as your DIP (rather than
Pinnacle), apply a −0.8% deduction to your computed
EV% before applying any tier threshold. This corrects
for the systematically noisier baseline.

SECTION 3 — BAYESIAN PROBABILITY FRAMEWORK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
True Bayesian updating requires three explicit components:

  PRIOR: Season-level performance distribution for each
  team/player on the relevant metric. Specify the prior
  as a Beta distribution for binary outcomes or a
  Gamma/Poisson for count outcomes. State prior
  parameters explicitly.

  LIKELIHOOD: The probability of observing the recent
  form data given each hypothesis. Recency-weighted
  observations enter the model here.

  POSTERIOR: The updated distribution after incorporating
  recent evidence. Report the posterior mean as the
  point estimate and the 90% credible interval as the
  uncertainty bound. For Thin Sample fixtures, widen
  this interval by 15% as specified in Section 1C.

  RECENCY WEIGHTING (exponential decay):
  Use weight w_t = exp(−λ × t) where t = games ago.
  λ (decay rate) is sport-specific:
    Soccer:  λ = 0.15
    NBA:     λ = 0.20
    Tennis:  λ = 0.25
    MLB:     λ = 0.10

SECTION 4 — SPORT-SPECIFIC MODELS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

── 4A. FOOTBALL (SOCCER) — DIXON-COLES POISSON MODEL ──

Step 1: Calculate attack and defense strength relative
to league average:
  Attack_i  = (Goals Scored_i per game) / League_Avg_Goals
  Defense_i = (Goals Conceded_i per game) / League_Avg_Goals

Step 2: Compute expected goals:
  xG_home = Attack_home × Defense_away × League_Avg × HFA
  xG_away = Attack_away × Defense_home × League_Avg
  HFA calibrated per league:
    La Liga ≈ 1.23 | Serie A ≈ 1.18 | EPL ≈ 1.15
    Bundesliga ≈ 1.20 | Ligue 1 ≈ 1.17 | MLS ≈ 1.25

Step 3: Apply Dixon-Coles low-score correction (τ)
for scorelines 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, 1-1.

Step 4: Augment with:
  - xG from recent fixtures (quality of chances)
  - xGA (chances allowed quality)
  - xT (Expected Threat) as build-up proxy

Step 5: Generate full scoreline probability matrix.
Derive 1X2, Over/Under, and BTTS from the matrix —
these must be internally consistent and must NOT be
modeled independently of one another.

── 4B. BASKETBALL (NBA / EUROLEAGUE) ──

Primary metrics (pace-adjusted per 100 possessions):
  - Offensive Rating (ORtg) and Defensive Rating (DRtg)
  - Net Rating = ORtg − DRtg
  - Four Factors: eFG%, TOV%, OREB%, FT/FGA
    (weighted 0.40, 0.25, 0.20, 0.15)
  - True Shooting %: TS% = PTS / (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA))

Matchup layer (mandatory):
  Map the favoured team's primary offensive action type
  against the opponent's coverage scheme.
  e.g., High P&R vs. Drop Coverage → quantify resulting
  3PA and mid-range frequency shift.
  Adjust ORtg by ±2–5 points for synergy/mismatch.

Player Props Volume Gate (weighted scoring — see 4B-i):

  [i]  Minutes Played ≥ 75% of season average
       (last 5 games)                         → 40 pts
  [ii] Usage Rate ≥ season average − 3%
       (last 5 games)                         → 35 pts
  [iii] Opponent DRtg against player's primary
        action type below league median        → 25 pts

  If [iii] data unavailable: award 12 pts and flag as
  "Matchup Data Unverified."

  PASS threshold: ≥ 60 points
  HARD DISQUALIFY: [i] AND [ii] both failing
  simultaneously (player is in a limited role —
  no edge can exist regardless of matchup).

  Do not override the hard disqualify condition.

── 4C. BASEBALL (MLB) ──

Pitcher evaluation — use ONLY:
  - FIP (Fielding Independent Pitching)
  - xFIP (FIP with regressed HR/FB rate)
  - SIERA (Skill-Interactive ERA) as tiebreaker
  - K%−BB% (Strikeout-Walk differential)
  Standard ERA is explicitly excluded from all models.

Bullpen state (72-hour lookback window):
  - Fatigued starter:       ≥45 pitches in prior 48h
  - Fatigued closer/setup:  ≥30 pitches in prior 48h
  A fatigued bullpen shifts run-line expectancy by
  +0.3 to +0.5 runs for the opponent in late innings.

Offensive inputs:
  wOBA and xwOBA, Barrel%, Hard Hit% — all split by
  pitcher handedness.

Weather gate:
  Wind >15mph toward CF or power alleys: +0.4–0.8
  expected runs → Over signal.
  Wind >18mph into hitters: Under signal.
  Apply weather adjustment to total before EV calc.

── 4D. TENNIS ──

Rating system: Surface-specific Elo only.
  Separate ratings for Hard, Clay, and Grass.
  Overall Elo is never used — only surface-specific
  rating matching the court being played on.

Serve/Return dominance model:
  Hold% and Break% (surface-specific, ≥5 matches floor)
  Point Win% on serve and return

Match win probability derivation:
  p = Hold%(A) / (Hold%(A) + Break%(B))
  q = Hold%(B) / (Hold%(B) + Break%(A))
  Use game-level Markov chain simulation to derive set
  and match probabilities from point level.
  The Markov chain is required — no top-down shortcuts.

EV-driven market selection:
  Calculate EV independently for:
    - Match Winner (ML)
    - Set Betting (2-0, 2-1, 3-0, 3-1, 3-2)
    - Game Handicap (−3.5, −4.5, etc.)
    - 1st Set Winner
  Recommend the market with the highest EV that clears
  the minimum threshold for its tier — not a preset format.
  If no market clears threshold, make no recommendation.

SECTION 5 — EV ENGINE: TIERED THRESHOLDS & CALCULATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

EV Formula:
  EV% = (True_Prob × Decimal_Odds − 1) × 100

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5A. TIERED EV THRESHOLDS (replaces single hard floor)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  TIER A — STRONG EDGE (proceed at full stake)
    Single bet:          EV ≥ +3.5%
    Safe Parlay leg:     EV ≥ +3.5%
    Value Parlay leg:    EV ≥ +5.0%

  TIER B — MARGINAL EDGE (reduced stake, flagged output)
    Single bet:          EV +2.0% to +3.4% → ½ normal stake
    Safe Parlay leg:     EV +2.0% to +3.4% → eligible only
                         if DCF ≥ 75 and MC Band ≥ 56%
    Value Parlay leg:    EV +3.5% to +4.9% → eligible,
                         flagged as MARGINAL in output

  TIER C — DISCARD (unchanged)
    Any EV < +2.0% → TYPE 1 PERMANENT DISCARD
    This floor is not negotiable.
    Sub-2% EV is noise, not edge.

  RECREATIONAL BOOK ADJUSTMENT:
    If DIP is sourced from a recreational book rather
    than Pinnacle, deduct −0.8% from computed EV%
    before applying tier thresholds. This corrects for
    the noisier baseline line.

  RATIONALE: Professional syndicates profit long-term at
  +1.5–2.5% EV against sharp markets. A +2.0% floor with
  a ½-stake penalty correctly captures real edge without
  discarding legitimate value. The original +4.0% floor
  was calibrated for idealized data conditions; the Tier B
  band handles real-world uncertainty through stake
  sizing rather than rejection.

For each recommended market, report all of the following:
  - Devigged Implied Probability (DIP)
  - Source of DIP (Pinnacle / sharp book / recreational)
  - Model True Probability (MTP) with 90% credible interval
  - EV% = (MTP × Decimal_Odds − 1) × 100
  - EV Tier: A or B
  - Sharp Book Consensus Line
  - Line Movement Direction: steam / reverse / flat

SECTION 6 — MONTE CARLO VARIANCE CHECK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For each recommended bet, perform a 10,000-trial Monte
Carlo simulation:

  [6a] Draw match outcomes from fitted Poisson / Elo /
       Markov distributions for the relevant sport
  [6b] Record whether the recommended market wins
       in each trial
  [6c] Compute win rate, standard deviation, and
       Kelly fraction from simulation output

Report for each bet:
  - Simulated Win Rate (must align with MTP within ±2%;
    divergence indicates model inconsistency — flag it)
  - Standard Deviation of outcomes
  - Monte Carlo Confidence Band
  - Recommended ¼ Kelly fraction:
      f* = (p × b − q) / b
      Stake = (f* / 4) × Bankroll

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6A. RISK-TIERED MC ADVANCEMENT GATES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Gates now scale with the risk tier of the bet, rather
than applying a single threshold universally:

  Very Low / Low Risk:
    MC Confidence Band ≥ 54%  AND  Kelly ≥ 0.025

  Medium Risk:
    MC Confidence Band ≥ 56%  AND  Kelly ≥ 0.030

  High Risk:
    MC Confidence Band ≥ 58%  AND  Kelly ≥ 0.035

  RATIONALE: The original 58% universal floor requires
  a 5.6pp cushion above break-even on a standard −110
  line (break-even = 52.4%). Lower-risk markets have
  tighter outcome distributions and do not require the
  same cushion as high-variance markets. Scaled gates
  correctly reflect this.

  If either MC condition fails for a bet's risk tier:
    → TYPE 1 PERMANENT DISCARD
    → Log MC Band and Kelly values that caused failure

SECTION 8 — PARLAY CONSTRUCTION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8A. INDEPENDENCE CHECK (mandatory before combining legs)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Legs from the same game are correlated and must never
be treated as statistically independent.

  Incorrect: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)
  Correct:   P(A ∩ B) derived from the joint scoreline
             probability matrix

If two legs originate from the same fixture, extract
their joint probability from the Dixon-Coles scoreline
matrix (soccer) or the possession-simulation model
(basketball). Failure to do this overstates true
parlay probability and understates true risk.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8B. SAFE PARLAY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Construction rules (all must be satisfied):

  - Maximum 4 legs
  - Every leg must clear:
      DCF ≥ 70
      EV Tier A (EV ≥ +3.5%) OR
      EV Tier B (EV +2.0–3.4%) with DCF ≥ 75 and
        MC Band ≥ 56% — flagged as MARGINAL in output
      MC Confidence Band meets its risk-tier gate
  - Maximum 2 legs per sport
      Both same-sport legs must be from different games
      (no same-game correlation)
  - No same-game correlated legs unless joint probability
    is derived from the scoreline matrix
  - No maximum odds cap — replaced by:
      MTP must exceed DIP by ≥ 3%
      Any leg above 2.30 decimal is flagged as
      "Elevated Variance" but not disqualified

  RATIONALE: The sport-limit change from 1 to 2 legs
  per sport allows parlay construction on heavy single-
  sport slates (e.g., full MLB or NBA days) without
  forcing invalid cross-sport combinations. The odds cap
  removal replaces an arbitrary ceiling with a genuine
  model-confidence requirement.

Report:
  - Cumulative True Probability (joint, corr.-adjusted)
  - Cumulative Implied Probability (from combined odds)
  - Overall Parlay EV%
  - ¼ Kelly Stake as % of bankroll
  - Monte Carlo Confidence Band for the combined slip
  - DCF Status for each leg
  - EV Tier for each leg (A or B, with B flagged)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8C. VALUE / ALPHA PARLAY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Construction rules:

  - Maximum 4 legs
  - Legs may include: derivative markets, Asian handicaps,
    alternate lines, player props (props only if Volume
    Gate in Section 4B scores ≥ 60 points)
  - Minimum individual leg EV:
      Tier A: ≥ +5.0%
      Tier B: ≥ +3.5% (flagged MARGINAL, stake halved)
  - Cumulative Signal Score must meet floor:
      3-leg parlay: ≥ 3
      4-leg parlay: ≥ 4
  - 🔴 SHARP DISAGREEMENT legs may be included only if
    conditions in Section 7B are met

Report:
  - Full EV decomposition per leg
  - EV Tier per leg (A or B)
  - Correlation adjustment notes for same-game pairs
  - Signal Score per leg and cumulative total
  - Risk Flag Summary: all 🔴 legs, direction of
    disagreement, and justification for inclusion

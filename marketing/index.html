Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to install anything?

No. EduSyncra runs in any web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Your school gets its own web address.

What happens after the free trial?

You'll be asked to subscribe to keep your access. If you don't, your data is safely held for a short grace period before removal — nothing is deleted the moment the trial ends.

Is my school's data private?

Yes. Every school has its own separate, encrypted database — your data is never mixed with any other school's.

Can parents and students use it too?

Yes — there's a parent portal, a public result checker, and SMS/WhatsApp communication built in.

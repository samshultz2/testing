How deductions work

  • Mark daily attendance under Attendance; entering a clock-in time auto-calculates minutes late and the naira deduction.
  • When you generate payroll for a month, each staff member's total lateness + absence deductions for that month are pre-filled into their payslip.
  • Already generated a run? Use “Refresh deductions from attendance” on the payroll page to pull the latest.

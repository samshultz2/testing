Download questions from myschool.ng — pick subjects & years, it saves straight to the bank

Scrapes past questions for the ticked subjects across the chosen years and saves them here, de-duplicated. Each question is auto-tagged with its section/topic and year. It runs in resumable steps in your browser — you can stop and resume any time. Tables are kept as text; a figure image is saved when the page exposes one, and questions that rely on a diagram we can't fetch are saved but held out of exams until you add the image (see the “needs images” review queue). Keep this tab open while it runs.

myschool draws most diagrams with JavaScript, so this server-side download rarely captures them. For diagram-heavy subjects, run the local waec_scraper.py --images (a real browser) and paste its output — captured figures show on the platform automatically.

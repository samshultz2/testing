How this works

Every bank question that refers to a picture it doesn't have — a diagram, figure, graph, map or circuit — flagged on import or worded around one. They're held out of exams until the picture is added. Fetch from myschool finds the question on myschool.ng and attaches its diagram; Upload adds your own (on a computer you can also drop or paste a picture); No image needed clears a false alarm.

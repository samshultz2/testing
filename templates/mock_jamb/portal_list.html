How the mock works
  • Your subjects appear as tabs. Move between them freely — you don’t have to finish one before the next.
  • Every answer saves as you go. If you lose network or close the page, just come back and resume.
  • On a computer, use A–D to answer, N/P for next/previous and S to submit — like the real CBT.
  • When time runs out, your paper submits automatically.

UTME score guide

  • 300+Top band — open to the most competitive courses.
  • 250+Strong — competitive for most courses.
  • 200+The usual minimum for most universities.
  • <200Keep practising — every mock moves you closer.

Exam-day tips

  • Answer every question — there’s no penalty for a wrong answer.
  • Flag tough questions and come back to them.
  • Aim for about 1 minute per question.

{# MathJax loader for Mock JAMB question text, options and passages. myschool (and manually-added questions) write inline math as \( ... \) and display math as \[ ... \] — e.g. \(\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\) or \(3x^2+2x-5\). We deliberately do NOT register $ ... $ as a delimiter, so a currency amount in a stem (e.g. "a good costs $5") is never mis-parsed as maths. Content is server-rendered, so MathJax's on-load auto-typeset covers every question; the helper below also re-typesets on demand for any dynamically inserted markup. #}

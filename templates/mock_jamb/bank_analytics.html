The classifier never invents a topic. Notation-only, figure-only or out-of-syllabus questions may stay untagged; ticking “make drawable” still lets them be used in exams (they just won't carry a topic label).

Tag with AI — uses your linked Anthropic key

Sends {{ subject.name }}'s bank questions to Claude, using the key set in Settings → OCR, and assigns each the best-matching JAMB syllabus topic. Re-tags the whole subject (or one year), overwriting existing topics — it only ever uses real syllabus topics, never invented ones.

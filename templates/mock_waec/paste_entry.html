Step 1: Turn a score sheet into rows with an AI

Take a photo of the marked score sheet (or recording sheet), upload it to any chatbot (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT…) with this prompt, then paste its answer into Step 2. No OCR, no API key.

Read this Mock WAEC score sheet. Output one line per student-and-subject that has a score, as comma-separated values in EXACTLY this order:

student, subject, score

- "student" is the admission number if shown, otherwise the student's full name.
- "subject" is the subject name exactly as written.
- "score" is the number out of 100.
- One line per subject per student. Skip blank/un-scored cells.
- Output only the rows. No headings, no totals, no extra text, no code block.

Example:
STU001, Mathematics, 72
STU001, English Language, 65

Step 2: Paste the rows

Paste one result per line: student, subject, score — optionally a 4th grade column. student is an admission number or name (middle names optional/shortened), subject may be an abbreviation (Eng, Maths, CRS…), and grade is auto-calculated from the score when omitted. Check the preview — admission numbers are the most reliable when names clash.
Example: STU001, Maths, 72  or  Jane Doe, Eng, 64

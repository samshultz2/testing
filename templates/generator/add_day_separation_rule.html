Add Day-Separation Rule

Keep a subject off two named days of the week together, for one class — if it lands on either day, it can't also land on the other that same week. It's still free on every other day, and free to be on just one of the two. This doesn't reduce the subject's total periods/week, it only rules out that one day-pairing.

Add Period-Placement Rule

Control where a subject can land in the day, for a class (optionally one specific arm). Doesn't change how many periods/week the subject gets, just where in the day each one is allowed to fall.

If the subject runs more than one period/week, every one of them must satisfy this rule on whichever days they're scheduled — a tight window (a single fixed period, or a narrow range) can only offer one slot per day, so a subject needing more periods/week than there are school days may become infeasible with a very tight rule.

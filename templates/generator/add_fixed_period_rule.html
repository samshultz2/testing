Add Fixed-Period Rule

Pin a subject to one specific period number for a class (optionally one specific arm) — whatever day it lands on, it must always be at that exact period. This doesn't change how many periods/week the subject gets, just where in the day each one falls.

If the subject runs more than one period/week, every one of them must land at this period on whichever days they're scheduled — so a subject needing 2 or more periods/week can only ever use up to 5 days (one occurrence per day at this period), and needs at least as many available days as periods/week to stay feasible.

Good to know

  • Correct is the letter A–D, or the answer's text.
  • True/False questions: fill only A and B.
  • Write formulas as LaTeX between \( and \), e.g. \(x^{2}\). The template has examples.
  • Difficulty is Easy, Medium or Hard (Medium if left blank). Marks defaults to 1.
  • Questions with a figure or diagram: add those one at a time on the bank page, where you can attach the picture.
  • Imported questions are in the bank straight away. Any exam can use them through Add from bank.

What should the sheet look like?
  • One row per subject: student_id, exam_year, subject, grade
  • Or one row per student: student_id, exam_year then a column for each subject, with the grade in it.
  • Grades A1 to F9. "ABS" (absent) is skipped. Subject names like “Maths” or “Eng” are understood.
  • student_id, exam_year, total_score, subject1, subject1_score … subject4, subject4_score
  • Total 0–400, each subject 0–100. If the total is blank, the four scores are added up.
  • Students are matched by admission number. A full name works only when exactly one student has it. Only students in your branch are matched.

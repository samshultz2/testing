How predictions work
WAEC from the mocks

Mock WAEC (and Mock JAMB subject scores) map to likely WAEC grades using historical patterns. 70+ typically means A1–B3.

JAMB from Mock JAMB

A weighted average of every Mock JAMB attempt, recent ones counting more, corrected by the measured mock→real bias. The range reflects consistency across attempts.

JAMB from WAEC

For students with WAEC results, each grade maps to an expected JAMB range, using the best four subjects.

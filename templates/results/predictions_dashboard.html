How Predictions Work

WAEC from Mock JAMB

We analyze a student's Mock JAMB subject scores and map them to likely WAEC grades using historical correlation patterns. Higher mock scores (70+) typically predict A1-B3 grades, while lower scores may indicate areas needing improvement.

JAMB from Mock JAMB

We use a weighted average of all Mock JAMB attempts, with more recent exams weighted higher. The prediction includes a confidence range based on consistency across attempts.

JAMB from WAEC

For students with WAEC results, we predict potential JAMB scores by mapping each WAEC grade to an expected JAMB score range and selecting the best 4 subjects.

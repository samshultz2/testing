How matching and merging work
  • Grouped when the surname is the same and the other names closely match — capitals, spelling slips and a missing middle name are ignored (“ABDULWAHAB ILUEDO ISMAIL” and “Abdulwahab Ismail”). Brothers and sisters with different first names are not grouped.
  • Merging moves everything saved for the other records — WAEC, JAMB, BECE / Common Entrance, mock exams, scores, class enrolments, attendance, fees — onto the one you keep, fills its blank details (e.g. middle name), then deletes the others. The kept record keeps its own admission number.

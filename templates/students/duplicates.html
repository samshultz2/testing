Merge Duplicate Students

Students with the same surname whose other names are the closest match (capitals, spelling slips and a missing middle name are ignored — e.g. “ABDULWAHAB ILUEDO AKHTAR-OZOMOYA ISMAIL” and “Abdulwahab Ismail”). Brothers and sisters with different first names are not grouped. For each group choose the record to keep — everything saved for the others (WAEC, JAMB, BECE / Common Entrance, mock exams, scores, class enrolments, attendance, fees …) is moved onto it, any blank details (e.g. middle name) are copied across, and the other records are deleted.

Merged two different pupils by mistake? Move their class records back (attendance, scores and enrolment).

How to get your keys (once):
  1. Create a free account at paystack.com and add your school’s bank account (that’s where the money settles).
  2. In the Paystack dashboard go to Settings → API Keys & Webhooks.
  3. Copy your Public Key (pk_…) and Secret Key (sk_…) and paste them below.
  4. Set the Webhook URL in that same Paystack page to the address shown below, so payments are confirmed automatically.

How parents pay once this is on:
  • Parents log into the parent portal, open their child’s fee bill, and tap Pay → they’re taken to Paystack to pay by card/transfer.
